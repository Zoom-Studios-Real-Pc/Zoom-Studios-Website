About Us

Learn more about our mission, our values, and the way we build.

Zoom Studios is an independent creative studio focused on building original software, games, AI systems, operating systems, and digital experiences. We believe that great projects come from combining creativity, technical skill, and passion.

Our goal is not only to make things that work, but to make things that matter. We are driven by innovation, self-improvement, and the courage to build ambitious ideas from the ground up.

We are also growing a community of people who want to create with purpose. If you enjoy programming, design, animation, 3D, drawing, or any other form of digital creation, there is a place for you here.

Join Our Team

If you want to join Zoom Studios, the first step is to contact us through Discord. Ask the Owner or a Team Member if they would accept you, and if they say yes, be ready to answer a few important questions: what would you bring to our team, what would you add to our projects, and how would you help us grow?

We are looking for people who can help with programming games, applications, operating systems, PC-related software, animation, modeling, drawing, and many other creative or technical areas. Do not be afraid to apply — we welcome people who are willing to learn, create, and contribute.

Most of our work is currently done for free while we build toward paid projects. When the projects are paid, contributors can receive compensation based on the work they helped create.
