✨ Who We Are

Building Original Digital Experiences

Zoom Studios is an independent digital studio focused on game development, desktop software, operating system projects, and creative tools. We aim to build software and games that are practical, engaging, and crafted with true independence.

👥 Join Us

Join Our Team

We are always open to creators who want to help build games, desktop software, 3D art, and original digital projects.

How to Join

  1. Join our Discord Server using the links on this page.
  2. Contact the Owner or Team Members and let us know you'd like to get involved.
  3. Tell us a bit about your skills: What would you like to build, what can you bring to our projects, and how would you like to contribute?
  4. Build together: Work with us on free projects; future commercial releases include fair compensation based on your contributions!
