# J.A.R.V.I.S APP

> A flexible interface for connecting J.A.R.V.I.S with powerful AI providers and unlocking advanced capabilities.

## Overview
J.A.R.V.I.S APP is the configuration layer that allows the assistant to connect with different AI providers depending on performance, cost, and use-case needs. It makes it easy to switch between models and choose the best setup for each task.

## Supported Providers
### 1. NVIDIA NIM
- Recommended for strong performance and cost-effective everyday use
- Excellent for fast responses and practical daily tasks

### 2. Google Gemini
- Great for large-context reasoning and multimodal tasks
- Suitable for detailed analysis, coding support, and complex workflows

### 3. OpenAI
- Best for advanced instruction-following and high-quality generation
- Ideal for creative and specialized tasks

### 4. Anthropic Claude
- Strong for coding, detailed writing, and deep reasoning
- Well suited for technical and structured problem-solving

### 5. xAI Grok
- Useful for alternative perspectives and dynamic conversational tasks

## Setup Guide
1. Open J.A.R.V.I.S APP and go to Settings > API Configuration.
2. Choose your preferred provider.
3. Enter your API key from the selected service.
4. Save the settings and test the connection.

## Recommendation
For most users, NVIDIA NIM or Google Gemini are the best starting point. For specialized high-end work, OpenAI or Claude can provide even greater depth and quality.
